# E-commerce Amazon Sales Analysis

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing a year of Amazon-style e-commerce sales, profit, order quantity, category performance, and customer acquisition trends across global markets.

Live Dashboard: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/nishchal7514/viz/E-commerceAmazonSalesAnalysis_Nishchal_Krishnappa/Dashboard1

## Overview

This dashboard tracks YTD performance across Sales, Profit, and Order Quantity, benchmarked against the prior year, and breaks performance down by category and market to surface where growth is coming from and where it's eroding.

## Key KPIs

| Metric | Value | YoY Change |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | $14.11M | Up 5.04% |
| Profit | $1.30M | Down 0.46% |
| Order Quantity | 0.16M | Up 15.02% |

## What's in the Dashboard

- Sales & Profit trend: Monthly bar/line charts (Jan-Dec) showing sales holding steady around $2.2M-$2.5M per month before dipping in Nov/Dec, while profit-per-order spiked in October before a sharp drop.
- - Order Quantity summary table: Sales and profit broken out month by month.
  - - Market share by sales (donut chart): LATAM leads at 45.86%, followed closely by Europe at 43.37%, with Pacific Asia (8.32%), Africa (2.25%), and USCA (0.21%) trailing far behind - highlighting a major white-space opportunity in USCA and Africa.
    - - Category-wise sales (bar chart with YoY %): Cardio Equipment ($2.34M) and Camping & Hiking ($1.66M) are the top categories; Camping & Hiking (+12.55%) and Water Sports (+21.10%) are the only categories growing YoY, while Fishing, Cleats, Women's Apparel, Men's Footwear, and Indoor/Outdoor Games are all down 20-26%.
      - - Customer Acquisition heatmap: Ranks each market (Pacific Asia, LATAM, Europe, Africa, USCA) by acquisition strength across all 12 months, showing LATAM and Europe consistently trading the top ranks while USCA and Africa lag.
        -
        - ## Insights Called Out on the Dashboard
        -
        - - Sales rose 5.04% to $14.11M, but profit fell 0.46% to $1.30M - a margin compression story worth digging into.
          - - Sales are strong in Cardio Equipment and Camping & Hiking, weak in Cleats and Men's Footwear - informs inventory and marketing prioritization.
            - - LATAM and Europe drive ~89% of sales combined; expanding USCA and Africa is the clearest growth lever.
